# Plant species A — tiers 1–2

Thirteen species in `src/plants/species/`, painted with the watercolour engine
(`docs/systems/paint.md`) through a small shared library in `src/plants/lib/small-*.ts`.
Each session grows one plant live, so every species is built to read as **one plant growing
continuously** across ~24 cross-faded growth steps (ADR 0060–0062).

Look at them: studio → **T1 · …** / **T2 · …** sections (growth stages + grow loop, generated by
`species.studio.ts`) and **Plants A · tier 1–2 review boards** (`plants-a-review`): `lineup-t1`,
`lineup-t2` (every species at full bloom, same world scale, on white with the paper multiplied)
and `strip-<id>` (one seed at growth `0 · .06 · .12 · .2 · .3 · .45 · .55 · .65 · .75 · .82 · .9 · 1`).
```
npm run shots -- --entry plants-a-review/lineup-t2
npm run shots -- --entry plants-a-review/strip-balloon-flower --seed 3
```

the water; anchor = the centre of its pad group on the surface). The five tier-1 flowers use
`SMALL_SIZE` = `PLANT_SIZES.small` (86×86) as their garden box but still draw in the 70-unit
`SMALL_DESIGN` box, scaled up by `plantFrame(p, def, opts, SMALL_DESIGN)` (`lib/small-size.ts`,
ADR 0125); maidenhair fern keeps 70×70. There is no species list: the review boards take tiers 1
and 2 from the registry, and each species calls `smallSuite` from `species/<id>.test.ts` (ADR 0251). `placeholder-sprout` is no longer a registered species: it is a
dev/test fixture in `src/dev/placeholder-sprout.ts` (used by unit tests; not offered to players).

## Growth staging (how every species is built)

1. **Plan first.** `plan<Species>(rng.fork('plan'))` (exported, pure) draws the whole plant —
   stems as turtle walks, leaf nodes, flower positions, petal rings, colourway, every
   growth window — consuming random numbers in a fixed order that never depends on growth.
2. **Render by growth.** `draw` maps `growth` to eased per-element progress on the shared
   timeline (`small-growth.ts`):

   | growth | stage |
   |---|---|
   | 0 – 0.12 | seed mound (earth wash + seed dab), first shoot breaks through (seed fades 0.06–0.16) |
   | 0.12 – 0.45 | stems extend (turtle stems scaled by `extent`, turns scaled by `bendK`); leaves unfurl one after another (`stagger`) |
   | 0.45 – 0.75 | buds form and swell, colour creeps in from green |
   | 0.75 – 1 | petals open, staggered per flower; full colour at 1 |

   Windows overlap a little so the plant never stands still; the water lily replaces the mound
   with a widening ripple ring and unrolling pads.
3. **Stable marks.** Every element paints with `rng.fork(label)` (and helpers split that again
   per engine call), so a leaf's brush marks are identical at every growth value — only
   size, angle and opacity move (ADR 0060).
4. **No popping.** Every size and opacity is a smooth function of growth; new marks start
   tiny *and* transparent (leaves/stems/blades fade in over their first few units); a bud
   dissolves into its opening petals inside one render (`bloom`, ADR 0062). This is enforced
   by a test (ADR 0061).

## Helper library (reuse these)

`small-growth.ts` — `STAGES`, `phase`, `ease`/`within` (smoothstep), `smoother`, `easeOut`,
`stagger(i, n, span, share)`, `swell`, `visible`.

`small-structure.ts` (pure, plant-local units: base at (0,0), up = −y) —
`makeStem`/`bendProfile`/`stemPoints(stem, extent, bendK)` (turtle stems that grow and
straighten/nod), `pointAt`, `subPath`, `pathLen`, `arc`, `add`, `lerpAngle`, `petalRing`,
`FlowerView {tilt, roll}`, `projectDir`, `petalPose(a, view, open, closedLen)` (a petal swings
from the closed bud direction to its foreshortened open direction).

`small-paint.ts` —
- `plantFrame(p, def, opts, design?)` sets world units with the base at the anchor (returns
  restore); `design` is the box the species is drawn in (default `def.size`).
- `groundShadow`, `seedMound`.
- `paintStem` (tapered stroke + optional broken ink over part of it), `paintLeaf` (dab with
  pooled shaded flank, cool shadow glaze, optional ink midrib; `k` unfurls it, `fold` swings it
  out), `dabAxis`.
- `toothedLeaf` (blade + tooth dabs + midrib bleed in one wet layer), `leafletFan` (compound
  toothed leaf unfolding in perspective — strawberry, anemone).
- `paintPetal` (own wet layer; `inner`/`innerWidth`/`tip` colours bleed wet-in-wet; `white`
  petals are painted source-over), `petalFlower` (radial flower in perspective, back to front),
  `paintBud` (body greens → colour, merged sepals), `bloom` (bud → flower dissolve),
  `smallBlossom` (tiny flower in one layer — forget-me-not), `stamens`.

`small-size.ts` — `SMALL_SIZE` (86, the garden box of the tier-1 flowers) and `SMALL_DESIGN`
(70×70, the box they are drawn in).

`small-recorder.ts` — `recordingPainter()` for node tests: logs every engine call with rng
streams replaced by seeds, back-fills layer opacity onto `dabInto` marks; `callNumbers`,
`callMagnitude`.

Conventions learned along the way: light comes from the upper left (`LIGHT`, pooled flank =
`shadowSide(angle)`); overlapping petals of one flower stay separate layers but tiny flowers and
sepals merge (overlaps knot up dark otherwise); petals pointing at the viewer must foreshorten
(`lerp(0.3, 1, pose.len)`), or perspective flowers sprout spikes; long arching blades are
strokes along turtle paths (a curved dab is only a parabola and never droops); opaque light
marks (white petals, the berry, eyes over petals) use `blend: 'source-over'` (ADR 0063).

## Tests
`src/plants/lib/small-growth.test.ts` — staging maths and pure structure (determinism, growth
continuity of `stemPoints`, projection). `smallSuite(id)` (`src/plants/lib/suites.ts`, called from
each `species/<id>.test.ts`, ADR 0251) — for each of the 13 species: well-formed definition (description under 80
characters, a native name, known habitats, tier 1 or 2); identical call log for the same seed + growth; different
for another seed; paints at every growth; and **continuous growth**: 200 growth steps × 2
seeds, no mark may move more than 5 % of the sprite between neighbouring steps, and marks may
only appear/vanish below 1.2 % of the sprite in size × opacity.

Vitest's file-system module cache is off (`fsModuleCache: false`, ADR 0004), so a new species
file is picked up by the registry's `import.meta.glob` without clearing any cache.
