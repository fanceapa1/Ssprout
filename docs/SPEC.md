# Study Sprout — Product Specification

Study Sprout is a browser study timer wrapped in a living Japanese watercolour garden.
The garden starts serene but sparse. Every completed study session blooms a new plant.
Over many sessions the garden fills, and wildlife slowly moves in.

**Guiding principle: subtlety.** Nothing shouts. No confetti, no badges, no streak guilt.
Rewards are quiet and beautiful, like a real garden changing over time.

> **Status notes.** Where the implementation differs from or does not yet meet a rule, the rule
> carries a *Status* note (audited 2026-10-22). `docs/TESTING.md` maps every rule here to the tests
> that cover it.

Reference mood: hand-painted Japanese watercolour / sumi-e on warm washi paper — soft
sage greens, grey-blue water, warm grey stones, muted pinks/violets/whites for flowers,
a small wooden pavilion, stepping stones leading to a pond. (Owner's reference image lives
outside the repo at `/Users/tudor/Downloads/image (1).png`; do not copy it, use it for mood.)

## 1. The garden (initial state)

- One fixed scene (not scrolling): sky wash, distant hills, two groups of thin-trunked trees
  (left, right), a wooden pavilion/engawa corner at the right, grassy banks, a central pond
  with boulders around it, a stepping-stone path from the bottom-centre to the pond.
- Barren but serene: a few grass tufts, a couple of shrubs. No flowers.
- Scene composition is responsive: landscape shows the full scene; portrait crops around
  the pond (focus point) without distorting. The garden is framed by the page's stage (§12),
  not the window.
  *Status:* cover scaling (`src/render/viewport.ts`) shows the whole 1600 × 1000 world only at
  16:10; other landscape aspects crop a little top/bottom or at the sides. Tall phones show at
  least 600 world units of width with paper above and below (ADR 0123). The sky above the hills
  is painted per daylight phase (§5). Plants are drawn 1.25× their v1 size in the garden (§3).

## 2. Study sessions — "every task is a plant"

The focus is the plant itself: **each study session is one plant**, chosen up front, that
grows live from seed to full bloom while you study. (Revised 2026-10-21, see ADR 0003.)

### Planting a session (start flow)
1. **Name the task** — free text, ≤ 40 chars (e.g. "Biology ch. 4"); placeholder suggestion;
   empty → "Study session". The name stays attached to the plant forever.
2. **Choose the time** — total session length, slider 10–180 min in 5-min steps, chips 25/50/90,
   remembers last value. The total decides the tier (see §3).
3. **Choose a study plan** — 2–4 recommended plans for that total, breaks *inside* the total.
   Always includes "Unbroken" (no breaks). Examples (breaks in brackets): 60 → "Pomodoro"
   (30 · (5) · 25), "Long focus" (25 · (10) · 25); 90 → "Pomodoro" (30·(5)·25·(5)·25), "Long focus"
   (40 · (10) · 40), "Deep work" (50 · (15) · 25); 150 → "Two deep blocks" (65 · (20) · 65).
   The exact catalogue lives in `docs/systems/core.md` (plans table). Generated by pure `recommendPlans(totalMinutes)`;
   every plan sums exactly to the total and always ends with a focus block. Sessions < 30 min
   only offer "Unbroken". The plan is shown as a small segmented brush line.
4. **Choose the plant** — browse every species whose tier ≤ the tier earned by the total.
   Higher-tier species are visible but locked with a hint ("needs 50 min+"); the earned tier's
   species are highlighted. Cards: painted preview, name, native name, poetic line, habitat.
5. **Choose its spot** — placement mode (see §3 Placement). Choosing an occupied spot = swap.
6. **Begin.** First start asks (gently) for notification permission.

### During the session
| Rule | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Growth | A seedling appears at the chosen spot and **grows live** in the garden and in the widget: growth = focused time ÷ total focus time (0 = seed, 1 = full bloom). Breaks don't grow it. |
| Focus blocks | Timer shows remaining time of the current block and of the whole session; plan segments show where you are. |
| Breaks = watering | At each break: soft notification "Time for a break — your <plant> is thirsty." The UI (and widget) show a watering can; **tap to water** → watering animation (can tilts, water arcs in droplets, soil darkens, leaves lift). Not watering has no penalty. "Skip break" is available (removes the rest of that break; the session ends that much sooner, tier unchanged). Next focus block starts automatically with a soft notification. |
| Pause | Unlimited, paused time doesn't count (focus or break). After 30 min paused, a soft "Still with us?" prompt offering Resume / End session. |
| Cancel | Light confirmation ("End this session? Your <plant> won't bloom this time."). The seedling gently withers and fades. A plant it was replacing returns. No penalty, counted in stats only. |
| Complete | When the plan finishes: the plant reaches full bloom, notification + chime (if unmuted), a quiet line "Your Japanese Iris for “Biology ch. 4” has bloomed." It becomes a permanent plant carrying its task name, minutes and date (shown on hover/tap). If it replaced a plant, the old one is pressed into the herbarium. |
| Persistence | Timestamp-based and saved. Reload/close and return → continues correctly (or completes if the plan ended while away). |

### Active-session ambience (subtle!)
While a session runs, the garden visibly "works with you":
- Weather variant chosen per session: **sun** (most common: soft diagonal light shafts, warmer
  light, gentle glints on water), **soft rain** (fine slanted strokes, ripple rings on the
  pond, slightly cooler/greyer palette), **mist** (low drifting haze).
- Photosynthesis: faint light motes rising slowly from existing plants; plants sway a touch more.
- Paused session: ambience fades to stillness (a held breath), not off abruptly.
- All of this is low-contrast and slow. It must never compete with the plants.

## 3. Rewards

Tier by **total session length** (chosen at start; gates which species can be planted):

| Tier | Goal minutes | Character | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10–24 | Small wildflowers, ground plants | violets, clover, forget-me-nots, wild strawberry, moss with tiny flowers |
| 2 | 25–49 | Classic perennials | Japanese iris, anemone, bellflower (balloon flower), dianthus, cosmos, spider lily |
| 3 | 50–89 | Flowering shrubs, showy flowers | peony, hydrangea, azalea, camellia, chrysanthemum, lotus (water) |
| 4 | 90–180 | Spectacular centrepieces | weeping cherry, Japanese maple, wisteria (on a trellis), pine bonsai-like, ginkgo |

*Status:* the species that exist (`src/plants/species/`, 25): tier 1 violet, white clover,
forget-me-not, wild strawberry, dandelion, maidenhair fern · tier 2 Japanese iris, anemone, balloon
flower, cosmos, red spider lily, silver grass, water lily · tier 3 tree peony, hydrangea, azalea,
camellia, chrysanthemum, lotus · tier 4 weeping cherry, Japanese maple, wisteria, black pine, ginkgo,
plum blossom. Not yet: moss and dianthus. **Bug:** the lotus (size class `large`) fits no water slot
(all are medium or small), so it is offered but cannot be planted
(tracked in `src/plants/plantable.test.ts`).

- The plant is chosen at session start from all species with tier ≤ earned tier (§2 step 4).
  There is no end-of-session picker.

### Placement
- The scene has ~50–60 predefined **slots** (not free placement), each with a habitat:
  `water` (on the pond: lotus, water lily), `shore` (pond edge: iris, reeds),
  `meadow` (grassy banks), `path` (beside stepping stones), `back` (far bank, for tall/trees),
  `rock` (crevices next to boulders, small plants).
- Each species lists allowed habitats. Only compatible slots are shown, as softly glowing seed
  markers. Each slot also has a max size class so trees only go where they fit.
- Occupied compatible slots can be chosen too → **swap**: while the session runs the old plant
  steps aside (fades out); on completion it is saved to the **Pressed Flowers** collection
  (a small herbarium book view); on cancel it returns.
- Choosing a higher-tier plant for an occupied slot is how "upgrades" happen.
- Growth is live during the session (see §2); finished plants sway gently.
- Plants in the garden are **1.25× their v1 size** (ADR 0148), painted at that size; slots are
  spaced so neighbours still read and nothing stands on the stepping stones or hides the engawa.
  The widget and the planting-sheet previews keep their own framing. Each plant casts a shadow
  away from the sun and catches a rim of light at sunrise and sunset (§5).
- Hover/tap a finished plant → tiny ink label: task name · minutes · date (the day the session
  started).

## 4. Wildlife (gradual, subtle)

Wildlife is unlocked by garden progress, introduced one at a time, rare at first. Animals behave
like real animals: koi drift and turn slowly, bees visit flowers that attract pollinators, birds
land on rocks then leave, dragonflies hover over water. Never more than a handful visible at once.
Unlock table (initial; tune in `src/core/fauna-unlocks.ts`):

| Unlocks at | Creature |
|---|---|
| always | water striders / tiny ripples, drifting leaf |
| 2 plants | dragonfly (occasional, near water) |
| 3 plants | first koi |
| 4 pollinator flowers | butterflies (cabbage white, swallowtail) |
| 6 pollinator flowers | bees visiting flowers |
| 7 plants | second koi |
| 8 plants | small birds (wagtail/sparrow) landing on rocks & path |
| any water plant + 10 plants | frog on a lily pad |
| 14 plants | swallows crossing the sky occasionally |
| 20 plants | grey heron, rare visit at the pond edge |
| 25 plants | third koi, more frequent birds |

When a new creature first appears, a single quiet line may appear ("A koi has found your pond.").
It is announced when it is first actually in view, not when it unlocks (ADR 0120); water striders
are never announced.

## 5. Time of day (daylight phases, ADR 0005)
The garden follows the system clock in **four painted phases — sunrise, midday, sunset, night** —
placed on **today's real sun** at a rough location guessed from the time zone (no permission
prompt, no network; a table of common zones, else the UTC offset's longitude). Each change is a
**40-minute blend** centred on its boundary:

| Boundary | Change |
|---|---|
| civil dawn (sun 6° below the horizon) | night → sunrise |
| sunrise + 60 min | sunrise → midday |
| sunset − 60 min | midday → sunset |
| civil dusk | sunset → night |

Short winter days keep midday around noon; white nights blend sunset into sunrise at solar
midnight; polar day stays midday, polar night stays night (with civil twilight: sunrise then
sunset around noon).

Each phase is painted (sky, light, shadows), not a filter:
- **Sunrise**: cool violet-blue overhead into a peach and apricot glow behind the hills on the
  right, rose-lit cloud banks, the hills cool and blue; the sun rises over the right-hand hills;
  mist lying on the pond and the far bank, lifting as the sun climbs; long soft shadows to the
  left; light on the right side of the crowns; dew glints on the near grass.
- **Midday**: a clear graded cerulean with a few clouds left as paper; the painting at its most
  saturated and crisp; short shadow pools under boulders, trees and plants; sparkle on the water.
- **Sunset**: rose-violet into crimson into an amber-gold glow on the left where the sun sinks
  behind the hills; crimson and gold cloud banks; warm rim light on crowns, boulders and plants;
  long violet shadows to the right; the pond reflecting the warm sky; the lantern and the shoji
  begin to glow.
- **Night**: a deep, granulating indigo (readable, never black or muddy), a painted moon showing
  its real phase arcing through the gap between the trees, a few faint stars, moonlight broken on
  the water, the lantern and a warm window glow; colours fade before they darken; plants remain
  readable.

Diurnal creatures rest while it is night (§4). Plants remain readable at all times. No seasons.

## 5a. Colour
Colours are richer than v1 (ADR 0144): greens, water, stones and flowers carry about 1.4× the
chroma, balanced by each phase's light so the painting stays coherent — still transparent
watercolour, never neon.

## 6. Timer visibility & notifications
- **In the page** (§12): the session controls live in the dock's Study section below the garden;
  on the garden a small **timer pill** shows the current block's time left with pause/resume and
  a way down to the controls (idle: a small "Start studying"). The tab title and favicon below
  keep the time when the page is elsewhere.
- **Floating widget** (Chrome/Edge): Document Picture-in-Picture window — a small painted
  card whose focus is **the session's plant**, rendered with its real species artwork and
  growing live from seed to bloom; below it the task name, remaining time, a progress line
  with plan segments, pause/resume, and during breaks a watering can to tap (watering
  animation plays in the widget and the garden). Opened by a button.
- **Tab title and favicon** (every browser; the only timer outside the page on Firefox/Safari):
  countdown in the tab title (`24:13 · Study Sprout`; also `Break 4:10 · …`, `Paused · …`,
  `Bloomed · …`) and a painted favicon that fills as time passes.
- **Notifications**: Notification API (via service worker registration when available) at
  completion, and at each break and back-to-focus (§2); only while the page is not visible and
  focused (ADR 0091). Works with tab in background. Closed tab → no notification (no server); the
  reward waits on return. Timer ticks use a Web Worker so background throttling doesn't delay
  completion.

## 7. Tooltips / onboarding
- Very subtle, small handwritten-feel hints near the relevant control (its home in the dock or
  on the garden, whichever is in view; §12), only for non-obvious
  features: duration slider/presets, the floating widget, placement (choose a glowing spot),
  swapping, pressed flowers, sound toggle. (Also: the task name, the study plan, watering and the
  notification question — 10 tips in `src/core/tips.ts`.)
- One tip at a time, the most relevant first; tips wait while the quiet line speaks (ADR 0124).
- Each tip shows until the user first uses that mechanism, then is retired. The small ⓘ lists
  every tip, the retired ones marked as learned.

## 8. Sound
Procedural Web Audio ambience (no audio files): soft water, wind in leaves, occasional distant
bird; light rain during rain sessions; a gentle bloom chime. **Muted by default**, speaker
toggle in the garden's corner and in the dock's Garden tools (one shared state); setting
remembered.

## 9. Persistence
- `localStorage` only (key `studysprout:v1`), versioned schema with migrations (the chain is
  in place; v1 is the only version so far).
- Export garden → downloads `study-sprout-garden-YYYY-MM-DD.json`; Import restores (validated).
- Hidden dev mode `?dev` exposes speed-up (`?dev&speed=60`; `?speed` alone is ignored) and a
  debug API for tests (`window.sprout`, docs/ARCHITECTURE.md › Dev / test hooks).

## 10. Platforms
Desktop-first; works on phones (touch, portrait crop, controls reachable: the dock stacks in one
column below the garden, so no panel covers the painting). Modern evergreen
browsers. Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (ambience/fauna reduced, no sway).

## 11. Developer tools
- **Studio** (`/Ssprout/studio.html`): a review page showing every sprite, creature, effect and
  paint primitive with seed/reseed, play/pause, speed, background and scale controls.

## 12. The app page (v2, ADR 0005; ADRs 0130–0136)
The page is no longer only a canvas. It scrolls as a normal document:

- **Stage.** The garden sits at the top with a small margin to the window's edges (14 px; 8 px on
  phones) and is ~88 % of the window's height on first load, so the top of the UI peeks out below.
  Its edges dissolve into the page's paper like a wash thinning out — broadly at the bottom, where
  the UI begins, and softly at the top and sides. The page is the same washi as the painting.
- **Dock.** Below the garden, sections registered by features, in a calm two-column layout on wide
  screens and one column on phones:
  - **Study** — the session controls: Start studying; task, block and total time, plan line,
    Pause/Resume, End with its gentle question, Water and Skip break in breaks, "Still with us?".
    The planting sheet is still a sheet over the page; choosing a spot brings the garden back
    into view.
  - **Your plant** — a large painted view of the session's plant, growing live, with the watering
    animation whenever it is watered; idle, the most recent bloom with its task, or an empty bed
    waiting for a first session. No guilt copy.
  - **Calendar** — reserved for the calendar (phase 8); hidden until it exists.
  - **Garden tools** — sound, floating timer, pressed flowers, garden data, hints, with icons and
    visible names; the same tools stay as icons in the garden's corner and share their state.
- **Timer pill** on the garden: time left in the block, pause/resume, and a way down to the
  controls; "Start studying" when idle. It and the corner icons rest (fade) while you focus.
- **Corner icons** are ~24 px ink glyphs on soft paper chips with ≥ 44 px targets, readable over
  any part of the painting at any time of day, labelled on hover and focus.
- **Scrolling.** Wheel (also over the garden), keyboard and in-page jumps move the page with a
  calm eased motion; touch scrolling is the platform's own; with `prefers-reduced-motion` there is
  no smoothing. A hand-made brush scrollbar on the right shows the position, can be dragged and
  fades when idle; it is decoration over normal scrolling (keyboard scrolling and focus are the
  browser's). ctrl/⌘ + wheel and pinch over the garden never scroll the page (reserved for zoom).
- **Type.** Bigger (base 21 px) and darker: body text ≥ 4.5:1 against the paper.

## Non-goals (v1)
Accounts, sync, server push, seasons, social features, streaks/penalties.
